Highlights

- NeuroCursor evaluates a randomized five-movement identity challenge.
- Five movements take a mean 4.43 seconds in the repeated-split evaluation.
- Strict logistic verification yields 98.78% mean AUC across 30 splits.
- CNN-GRU does not outperform GRU-only on the fixed neural comparison.
- Unseen-impostor and enrollment controls expose limits to generalization.
