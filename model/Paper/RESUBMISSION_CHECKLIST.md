# Resubmission checklist

## Completed technical and editorial preparation

- Concise Introduction and Related Work; hypotheses, four contributions, and
  comparative strengths/limitations retained. No arbitrary page-count target.
- Eight point-by-point reviewer responses; page/section/table locations checked.
- Model/input definitions, fixed hyperparameters, calibration, session isolation,
  known versus excluded impostors, enrollment/order/feature controls, uncertainty,
  reporting, and data/code access are documented.
- Tables, figures, bibliography, original evidence, tests, and archive contents
  verified. Existing AI disclosure preserved byte-for-byte.
- Corrected editable highlights supplied. Do not reuse the older root-level
  Highlights PDF: its temporal-learning claim exceeds the current evidence.

## Author/institutional actions required before uploading

1. Obtain a written institutional clarification of the study's OHSP/IRB status.
   No formal approval, exemption, waiver, or retrospective approval is assumed.
   Use the institution's actual determination and reference/date to update both
   the ethics paragraph and response letter. The current files explicitly say
   that written clarification has not been supplied; do not submit them while
   representing that requirement as resolved.
2. Have all coauthors review and approve this exact revision, author contributions,
   data-sharing statement, affiliations, and AI disclosure.
3. In the journal portal, verify the decision letter's required revision files,
   anonymization rules, editable-source/figure format, and declarations/forms.
   The official author-guide page returned an access error during this check;
   journal-specific file requirements were not certified.
4. Replace the old August 31 initial-submission cover letter with a revision
   letter identifying HCCOM-D-26-00263 and the enclosed responses. Confirm the
   current preprint/originality declarations and final author approval rather
   than copying assertions from the old letter without confirmation.
5. Check the separate title page, if requested, against the finalized ethics
   statement. Its older wording about academic approval is not a substitute for
   the written institutional determination.

No journal submission or institutional approval has been performed on the
authors' behalf. No further institutional contact or email is authorized.
